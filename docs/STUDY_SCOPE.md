# Study scope and evidence status

**Project:** BLUESHIELD FILTER  
**Authors:** Sabik Bin Sultan and Shafi Bin Sultan

BLUESHIELD is an exploratory stakeholder-survey study with a secondary treatment concept and preliminary engineering assessment. The repository supports checking reported aggregate arithmetic and reproducing explicitly assumed design scenarios. It does not contain respondent-level data or a validated treatment prototype.

## Available evidence

| Material | What it supports |
|---|---|
| Reported percentages, counts and original graphics for 125 participants | Description of the recruited sample and checking aggregate arithmetic |
| Reported category/agreement counts for a 25-professional interview subsample | Identifying reported implementation concerns; these are not transcripts or a recoverable coding dataset |
| Assumed inputs and nine engineering equation groups | Conditional calculations concerning batch timing, membrane area, pressure, UV exposure and pumping work |
| Original drawings and conceptual stage/installation models | Explaining intended components and connections |
| Cited component studies and credited external figures | Relevant mechanisms and prospective test questions; results remain those of the cited studies |

The reported sample contains 75 participants from Bangladesh and 25 each from India and Pakistan, comprising 70 students and 55 professionals. Environmental science and civil engineering account for 95 of the 125 participants. These totals describe the sample, not national representativeness.

The principal favorable ratings are 84.0% for perceived technical feasibility, 86.4% for anticipated effectiveness and 92.0% for monitoring usefulness. Their equivalents of 105, 108 and 115 responses are conditional calculations using a denominator of 125, not recovered respondent records.

## Missing study documentation

Participant responses, the complete questionnaire, recruitment details, collection dates, interview transcripts, and consent or ethics documentation have not been recovered.

- Sampling probabilities, response rates, item-level missingness and recruitment bias cannot be reconstructed.
- Country/occupational comparisons, response correlations, reliability analysis and participant-level models cannot be inferred from these summaries.
- Interview coding reliability, saturation and links between transcripts and category counts cannot be independently verified.
- No ethics approval, exemption or consent procedure is asserted.

STROBE (manuscript key **r15**) and COREQ (**r16**) are reporting references. Citing them does not establish that the original collection satisfied their recommendations. Future collection requires its own documented procedures.

## Unresolved impact item

The narrative and summary give social, environmental and economic shares of **65.6%, 21.6% and 12.8%**. The manuscript records a conflict with the retained impact pie chart. The pie-chart percentages have not been independently verified for this repository and are not transcribed into its data files. Underlying response records and denominator definitions are unavailable.

The narrative values and the unresolved conflict are retained for traceability. Neither source is selected as authoritative, and the item is excluded from substantive analysis. The impact JSON records the chart percentages and selected distribution as null. No respondents or replacement percentages should be invented.

## Treatment concept and limits

The proposed sequence is screened intake, controlled coagulation and flocculation, settling and sludge removal, a supported graphene oxide cartridge, terminal UV-C treatment, and protected storage. Monitoring supports process supervision and fault response.

The GO stage requires a defined material and support architecture. Published results do not establish universal removal, salt rejection or stability for BLUESHIELD. UV performance depends on delivered exposure and reactor conditions; electrical lamp wattage alone does not establish microbial inactivation. Physicochemical sensors do not directly certify microbial or chemical safety.

Models illustrate intended arrangements and service access. They are not manufactured prototypes, dimensioned fabrication drawings, flow simulations or optical simulations. Reproduced research figures remain external evidence; their credits and reuse terms are retained in the manuscript and provenance record.

No BLUESHIELD removal efficiency, microbial log reduction, potability, membrane life, sustained throughput, total energy consumption, operating cost or household adoption has been measured. No WHO household-water-treatment performance classification is assigned. The illustrative 40 mJ/cm² study fluence is not a universal safety threshold or approved setpoint.

Expansion toward at least 350 participants remains a future recruitment target requiring a justified sampling and study-size plan. More responses alone cannot correct selection bias or validate the device. Intended users and operators, source-water variability, maintenance and sustained use remain evaluation subjects.
